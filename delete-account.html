Delete your Movla account

Movla workout and nutrition app

Deleting your account permanently removes, straight away:

  • your account (email and password)
  • everything synced to it: profile, plans, workouts, food log, weigh-ins, measurements, activity summaries, pain reports and settings
  • coach links, messages and plans sent through Movla

Nothing is kept afterwards, except a report you filed about another user, which we keep without your account details until it's reviewed. Data saved only on your phone stays there until you erase it in the app (Settings → Erase all data) or uninstall the app.
