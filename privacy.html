Movla Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · updated for v1.14 · Terms of Use

The short version: Movla has no ad networks, no ad tracking and no analytics, and works fully without an account. The free plan shows one small card at a time (our own tips, or a plain product link); showing it sends nothing anywhere. If you create an optional account, your data is backed up privately so you can use it on more than one device. We don't sell or share your personal data, and you can delete your account at any time.

What the app stores, and where

Your profile (age, height, weight, goal), workouts, food log, body-weight entries, meal plans, household members and settings are saved in the app's storage on your phone or in your browser. Without an account, that is the only place they exist: nobody else, including us, can see them, and deleting the app or using Settings → Erase all data removes them.

Backups are files you create and save yourself (for example to Files, Google Drive or email). We never receive them.

Accounts and cloud sync (optional)

You can create an account with your email address and a password (Settings → Account & sync). When you're signed in, a copy of your app data (profile, plan, workouts, food log, weigh-ins, measurements, pain reports, settings and, if connected, daily step, sleep and heart-rate summaries) is stored in your account so you can restore it on another device.

Account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest. Database rules allow only you to read your data, except what you choose to share with a coach (below). We don't use it for advertising and don't sell it.

Deleting your account: Settings → Account & sync → Delete account permanently removes your account and all of its cloud data, including coach links, straight away. Data on your phone stays until you erase it. Signing out keeps your cloud data so you can sign in again later.

Consumer health data

Some of what you enter is health data under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act: body weight and measurements, calorie and nutrition logs, workouts, pain reports, answers to the health check, and step, sleep and heart-rate summaries from Apple Health or Health Connect.

  • Why we collect it: only to provide the app: your plan, targets, progress and, if you choose, coaching. Never for advertising, and never sold.
  • Your consent: without an account this data never leaves your device. When you create an account we ask for your consent before storing it in your account; we record when you agreed.
  • Who receives it: our database provider (Supabase), which processes it only for us, and a coach only if you link with one and only the categories you choose. Nobody else.
  • Your rights: see your data any time in the app, and export all of it with Settings → Back up your data. Withdraw consent and delete everything with Settings → Account & sync → Delete account (or on the account deletion page). Change or stop coach sharing in Settings → Coaching. We don't charge for any of this.

Messages and reports

Messages between you and your coach are stored with your account, readable only by the two of you, and deleted with either account. If you report a message, a copy of that message and your reason are kept for review so we can act on it.

Apple Health and Health Connect

If you connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), Movla reads your steps, active calories, sleep, resting heart rate, body weight, body fat percentage and workouts recorded by other apps, watches and smart scales, and writes the body weight you log in Movla. This data is used only to show your activity and recovery in the app. If you have an account, daily summaries (steps, active calories, sleep minutes, resting heart rate) are included in your private cloud copy; individual samples are not. It is never used for advertising, and is never sold or shared with anyone. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app (iPhone) or Health Connect settings (Android), or in Movla's Settings.

Working with a coach

With accounts: you join a coach by entering their invite code and choosing what they can see: training, daily nutrition totals (never individual foods), body (weigh-ins, measurements, age, height), phone and watch activity, and pain and health flags. Your coach can then read only those categories, enforced by the database, and only while you're linked. You can change the choices, pause all sharing or leave at any time in Settings → Coaching, and the change applies immediately. Your coach can send you plans and nutrition goals, which change nothing until you accept them. Messages between you and your coach are stored with your account, readable only by the two of you, and deleted with either account.

Without accounts: if you link with a coach using an invite file, nothing is sent automatically. When you tap Send update, the app creates a file with your last 4 weeks of workouts, daily food totals, weigh-ins, measurements, any pain reports and, if you've connected Apple Health or Health Connect, your 7-day step and sleep averages, and you choose how to send it (message, email, AirDrop). After the invite, these files are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key only you and your coach's app hold. A coach's copy of your data lives only on their device, and you can unlink at any time.

Photos and backups

Movla doesn't take, store or upload photos of you. (Earlier test versions had an on-device progress photo feature; it was removed, and updating the app deletes any photos it stored.) The camera is only used to scan food barcodes, below. Backups can be protected with a password; protected backups are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and can't be opened without it.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan food barcodes, and only when you tap Scan. Images are processed on your device and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Notifications

Reminders, and alerts about new coach messages, are created on your device by the app itself. No push notification service is involved.

Children

Movla is for people 16 and older and isn't directed at children. Parents can add children to a household meal plan to size portions; that's only a name, age and calorie estimate, kept with the parent's data (on the device, and in the parent's account if they have one).

Health information

Movla gives general fitness and nutrition guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet program, especially if you have a health condition or are pregnant.

Changes and contact

If we change what the app collects, this policy will be updated before the change ships. Exporting and deleting your data are built into the app (see Consumer health data above). Other questions: open an issue on GitHub (please don't include personal details there).
