Groundwork Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

The short version: Groundwork has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.

What the app stores, and where

Your profile (age, height, weight, goal), workouts, food log, body-weight entries, meal plans, household members and settings are saved only in the app's storage on your phone or in your browser. Nobody else, including us, can see it. Deleting the app or using Settings → Erase all data removes it.

Backups are files you create and save yourself (for example to Files, Google Drive or email). We never receive them.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan food barcodes, and only when you tap Scan. Images are processed on your device and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device by the app itself. No notification service or server is involved.

Children

Groundwork is not directed at children under 13. Parents can add children to a household meal plan; that information stays on the parent's device like everything else.

Health information

Groundwork gives general fitness and nutrition guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet program, especially if you have a health condition or are pregnant.

Changes and contact

If a future version adds accounts or cloud sync, this policy will be updated before that feature ships and you'll be asked before any data leaves your device. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
